01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE SYSTEMS

Technology should
make more possible.

I’m a Computer Science professional-in-training focused on AI engineering, language technology, and intelligent systems. My work brings together machine learning, LLMs, and practical software development.

A major part of my work is centered on Balochi, a low-resource language that deserves better digital tools. I work across the pipeline: preparing data, building tokenizers, adapting language models, training NLP systems, and exploring useful applications.

Beyond language technology, I experiment with agentic workflows, retrieval systems, and AI-powered products. I care about the engineering behind the demo: reproducibility, evaluation, and whether a tool solves a real problem.
